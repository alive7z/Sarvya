# Sarvya extension privacy disclosure

The extension processes the active page locally only after you press Scan. It reads the URL, title, DOM snippets, selectors, accessibility rule results, and rule counts. Findings may contain page text or personal information present in the DOM. Do not scan sensitive pages unless you are authorised and comfortable retaining that evidence in your browser session.

No data is transmitted to the developer, the Sarvya server, analytics services, or AI providers. The extension does not request cookies, browsing history, credentials, or blanket host access. Opening an external axe rule-guidance link is an explicit user action that navigates to the documentation provider.

Results are stored in Chrome session storage, not synchronised storage. They are cleared on tab navigation/close, browser restart, or Clear results. Downloaded JSON reports remain wherever you save them until you delete them yourself. The temporary inspection overlay disappears after 15 seconds, scrolling/resizing, or its Close button.

This document describes the checked-in implementation. Complete store-specific privacy declarations from the same behaviour before submission. No store listing or support contact has been fabricated.
